Getting Here

  • DART/IrishRail: Pearse Station — 200m from Chartered Accountants House. Has lifts; check status on the day.
  • Luas Green Line (recommended): Northbound: Dawson Street stop is 900m from venue. Southbound: Trinity stop is 700m from venue. Fully step-free with level boarding.
  • Dublin Bus: 100% low-floor fleet. Stops on Nassau Street and College Green.
  • Blue badge parking: ~10 disabled bays on Trinity College Dublin campus. Enter via Lincoln Gate (Lincoln Place) and ask security for the nearest bay. First-come, no pre-booking.
