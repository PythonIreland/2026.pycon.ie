Getting Here

  • DART / IrishRail: Dublin Pearse Station — 200m from Chartered Accountants House. Has lifts; check status on the day.
  • Dublin Bus: 100% low-floor fleet. Stops on Pearse Street, Townsend Street, and Westland Row.
  • LUAS Green Line: Northbound: Dawson Street stop is 900m from venue. Southbound: Trinity stop is 700m from venue. Fully step-free with level boarding.
  • Taxi: You can use the Tap a Taxi app to book a taxi.
  • On-site parking: Some parking may be available underneath Chartered Accountants House. Email us at {{ .Site.Params.email }} to see what can be arranged.
  • Blue badge parking: ~10 disabled bays on Trinity College Dublin campus. Enter via Lincoln Gate (Lincoln Place) and ask security for the nearest bay. First-come, no pre-booking.
  • Further Information: Travelling on public transport with a disability or reduced mobility.
