# PyCon Ireland 2026 - Sponsorship Prospectus

**Autumn 2026 | Organized by Python Ireland**

---

## Overview

PyCon Ireland 2026 is a one-day, multi-track conference featuring talks, tutorials, and workshops, bringing together Python enthusiasts from across Ireland and beyond. 

* **Duration:** 1 Day
* **Attendees:** ~500 Guests
* **Format:** Multiple Tracks (Talks, Workshops, Tutorials, Entertainment)

Your conference ticket grants access to all activities, including breakfast and lunch. On Saturday evening, join us for a fun and relaxed entertainment night — perfect for networking and unwinding.

---

## Attendees Profile

PyCon Ireland appeals to a wide range of backgrounds, including:
* Software Developers
* Data Scientists
* Machine Learning Researchers
* Students
* Entrepreneurs

With attendees ranging from aspiring programmers to experienced industry leaders, PyCon Ireland provides a unique opportunity for sponsors and recruiters to connect with top tech talent, showcase their brand, and engage directly with the Python community.
